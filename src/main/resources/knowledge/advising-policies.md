# SJSU Campus Advising Center – Policies and FAQ

(Synthetic knowledge base written for the CMPE 172 term project. Each "##" section is one document in the vector store.)

## Office hours and location
The Campus Advising Center is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and closed on weekends and university holidays. Dr. Sarah Kim (Computer Science) meets students in MacQuarrie Hall 215. Raj Patel (Computer Engineering) meets students in Engineering 284. Appointments start on time; please arrive 5 minutes early.

## How to book an appointment
Log in with your student account, open Available Slots, filter by advisor, service, or date, and choose a slot. You can also ask the AI assistant to find and book a slot for you; it will always show a summary and ask you to confirm before it books anything. Each slot can be booked by only one student. New slots are usually published up to two weeks in advance.

## Advising services
Registration Help (30 minutes): planning your schedule and registering for classes. Major Change (45 minutes): requirements and steps for changing your major. Graduation Check (30 minutes): reviewing your remaining graduation requirements. Probation Advising (60 minutes): support and an academic success plan for students on academic probation.

## Cancellation policy
You can cancel an upcoming appointment yourself from My Appointments or by asking the AI assistant. Please cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment so another student can use the slot. Past appointments cannot be cancelled. Cancelled appointments stay in your history.

## No-show policy
If you miss an appointment without cancelling, it counts as a no-show. After three no-shows in one semester, you must call the advising office to book future appointments instead of booking online.

## Late arrival
If you arrive more than 10 minutes late, the advisor may ask you to reschedule because the next student's appointment cannot be delayed.

## What to bring
Bring your SJSU student ID and an unofficial transcript or degree progress report. For Registration Help, bring a list of classes you are interested in. For a Graduation Check, bring your completed graduation application draft. For Probation Advising, bring your most recent grade report and any academic success plan you already have.

## Registration holds
If you have a registration hold, an advisor can explain what the hold is for, but only the office that placed it can remove it. Advising holds are usually removed within one business day after your advising appointment.

## Graduation application
Students should apply for graduation one year before their expected graduation term. Book a Graduation Check before you submit the application so an advisor can confirm your remaining requirements.

## Notifications and reminders
You receive a confirmation message when you book and when you cancel an appointment. An automated assistant also sends a personalized reminder about one day before each upcoming appointment. If the notification system is temporarily unavailable, your booking is still saved and the message is delivered later.

## Virtual appointments and accessibility
Any appointment can be held over Zoom if you request it in the notes when booking. Students who need accommodations can contact the Accessible Education Center, and advisors will follow approved accommodation letters.

## Contact
Advising office email: advising@sjsu.edu. Phone: (408) 555-0172. For urgent issues outside office hours, email the office and an advisor will reply within one business day.
